Patient Medical History Summarisation

I collaborated with my team to develop a website created to provide an efficient method for doctors to read summarised patient medical notes by using NLP techniques. The website enables healthcare professionals to view a patient's medical timeline accompanied by summarised notes, generated with the BioGPT and BART text models from Hugging Face Transformers. Key features include a Patient Details summary and Discharge and Radiology timelines and notes.
